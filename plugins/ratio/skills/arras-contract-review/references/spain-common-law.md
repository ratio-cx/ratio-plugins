| The debtor cannot free itself from performing by paying the penalty unless that right was expressly reserved; the creditor cannot claim both performance and the penalty unless clearly granted | 1153 | [art. 1153](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1889-4763#art1153) |
| The judge moderates the penalty when the obligation was partly or irregularly performed | 1154 | [art. 1154](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1889-4763#art1154) |

**How courts read unclear arras.** Established Supreme Court case law treats art. 1454 as an exception and reads it restrictively: arras are penitential only when the contract says so clearly and unequivocally (naming art. 1454 or describing the right to withdraw by losing / returning double). Otherwise the payment is read as **confirmatory**: an advance on the price, and the art. 1124 remedies apply (the other party can force the sale or terminate and claim damages). That default applies when the contract agrees neither a right to withdraw nor a penalty. If a clause expressly sets a penalty (the deposit is lost on breach) without a right to withdraw, read it as **penal arras** (arts. 1152-1154), and check whether the wording also lets the other party force the sale (art. 1153). This is a summary of a line of case law; no single judgment was verified against CENDOJ, so in a report its origin is "case-law line, specific judgments not verified".

### What that means for the money

| Type | Buyer walks away | Seller walks away | Mortgage refused, no financing condition applies |
|---|---|---|---|
| Penitential (art. 1454) | Loses the deposit, nothing else | Returns it doubled, nothing else | Buyer is walking away: loses the deposit |
| Confirmatory (default) | Seller can force the sale or terminate and claim damages (the deposit counts towards them) | Buyer can force the sale or terminate and claim damages | Buyer is in breach: exposed to art. 1124 remedies |
| Penal | Loses the deposit as a penalty; whether the seller can also force the sale depends on the wording (art. 1153) | Same, mirrored, only if the clause is mirrored | As breach |

The last column assumes no financing condition applies. A financing condition that is not in the contract gives the buyer no recorded protection; if the user says one was agreed orally or later, do not apply that column as a verdict: state both outcomes and leave whether the agreement binds and can be proved to the lawyer. The common Código Civil has no rule like Catalonia's art. 621-49.

## New build and off-plan

Ley de Ordenación de la Edificación (Ley 38/1999), consolidated text updated 15/06/2022: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1999-21567

| Rule | Article | Source |
|---|---|---|
| A developer that takes money from buyers for the construction of homes must guarantee its return plus legal interest, from the building licence, with a surety insurance (seguro de caución) or a joint and several bank guarantee (aval solidario), in case construction does not start or is not completed within the agreed delivery period | LOE DA 1ª, Uno.1.a | [DA 1ª](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1999-21567#daprimera) |
| The money must be paid into a special account at a credit institution, separate from the developer's other funds, and used only for the construction | LOE DA 1ª, Uno.1.b | same |
| The guarantee covers the amounts paid, including taxes, plus legal interest | LOE DA 1ª, Uno.2 | same |
| Insurance: one individual policy per buyer, identifying the home | LOE DA 1ª, Dos.1.a | same |

What to check in an off-plan contract: the guarantee is named (insurer or bank), it is individual, it covers taxes and interest, and payments go to the special account. Missing any of these is **serious** for a buyer.

## Cash payments

Ley 7/2012, consolidated text updated 10/03/2022: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2012-13416

| Rule | Article | Source |
|---|---|---|
| When any party acts as a business or professional, an operation of €1,000 or more (€10,000 for an individual payer without tax residence in Spain not acting as a business) cannot be paid in cash, **not even in part**: the threshold is the amount of the operation, and split payments are added up. A €500 cash reservation towards a flat bought from a developer is already a breach | 7.Uno.1 | [art. 7](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2012-13416#a7) |

Between two private individuals the limit does not apply, but a cash deposit leaves no trace: recommend a bank transfer, and in the deed the means of payment must be identified.
