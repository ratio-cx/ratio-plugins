# Terms for deposit contracts

The terms this skill uses in what the user sees, with the same rules as the shared glossary: `translate`, `translate-gloss`, `keep-es`, `keep-acronym`. The shared glossary still applies to everything else (nota simple, formatting of numbers and dates). Where both define an English word, this file wins inside this skill: here "deposit" is never the auction depósito of the shared glossary.

## Ratings

| EN | ES | CA | Definition | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Serious | Grave | Greu | The user can lose money or be forced into a sale they cannot complete, a legal requirement is missing, or the clause may be an unfair term | translate |
| Negotiable | Negociable | Negociable | Unbalanced or vague, worth asking to change, not a deal breaker | translate |
| Fine | Correcto | Correcte | Correct and balanced | translate |

## Contract types

| EN | ES | CA | Definition | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| deposit contract (arras) | contrato de arras | contracte d'arres | Contract in which the buyer pays a sum to the seller before the deed, binding both to the sale | keep-es |
| penitential arras | arras penitenciales | arres penitencials | Arras that let either party walk away: the buyer loses them, the seller returns them doubled | keep-es |
| confirmatory arras | arras confirmatorias | arres confirmatòries | Arras that are only an advance on the price: a party that walks away is in breach | keep-es |
| penal arras | arras penales | arres penals | Arras that work as a penalty for breach, without a right to walk away | keep-es |
| reservation agreement | contrato de reserva | contracte de reserva | Usually an agency document that takes the property off the market for a sum; often not a sale commitment | translate-gloss |
| the deposit (the sum paid under the contract, in prose) | las arras, la señal, el importe entregado | les arres, el senyal, l'import lliurat | Use the word the contract uses; never "depósito" | translate |
| deed of sale | escritura pública de compraventa | escriptura pública de compravenda | The notarial deed that completes the sale | translate-gloss |
| financing condition | condición de financiación | condició de finançament | Clause that lets the buyer withdraw and recover the deposit if the mortgage is refused | translate |
| surety insurance | seguro de caución | assegurança de caució | Insurance that guarantees the return of amounts paid on account for an off-plan home | translate-gloss |
| bank guarantee | aval bancario | aval bancari | Bank guarantee for the same purpose | translate-gloss |

## Regional names

The user may call the document by another name. Recognise these, use the user's word when quoting the contract, and say once which figure it corresponds to:

| Term | Where it is used | Closest figure |
|---|---|---|
| seña | Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay | arras or deposit; the type depends on the wording |
| boleto de compraventa | Argentina, Uruguay | preliminary sale contract, often with a seña |
| promesa de compraventa, contrato de promesa | Chile, Colombia, Mexico, Peru and others | preliminary contract committing both to sign the sale |
| reserva, contrato de reserva | Spain and Latin America | reservation agreement |
| arras, contrato de arras | Spain, Chile, Colombia, Peru | deposit contract |
| contracte d'arres | Catalonia | deposit contract |

Outside Spain this skill has no reference for the local law: the regional name tells you what the document is, not which rules apply.
