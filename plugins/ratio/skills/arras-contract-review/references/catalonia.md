# Catalonia, and the other territories with their own civil law

**Generated on 9 Nov 2026** from the consolidated texts of the BOE. When citing a row in a report, its origin is "reference file (generated on 9 Nov 2026)", unless you verified it again online in this conversation.

## Catalonia

For a property in Catalonia, the sale is normally governed by the Catalan Civil Code (Codi civil de Catalunya, CCCat) rather than art. 1454 of the common Código Civil, unless the contract chooses another law; whether that choice is valid is for the lawyer. Community debts follow art. 553-5 below, not art. 9.1.e LPH. The housing law below (Ley 18/2007) depends on where the property is, so apply it to any property in Catalonia, whatever law the contract chooses. State laws still apply: the off-plan guarantees (LOE DA 1ª), the cash limit (Ley 7/2012, art. 7), IBI and plusvalía, including the buyer's role when the seller is a non-resident individual (TRLRHL). Take them from [spain-common-law.md](spain-common-law.md). Catalonia adds its own housing law, below.

### Arras and financing

Book Six (Ley 3/2017, contracts), consolidated text, last modified 16 Dec 2021: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2017-2466 (PDF: https://www.boe.es/buscar/pdf/2017/BOE-A-2017-2466-consolidado.pdf). In force since 1 Jan 2018; the State's challenge against the sale articles was decided by STC 132/2019, and arts. 621-8 and 621-49 remain in the consolidated text.

| Rule | Article |
|---|---|
| **Default is confirmatory.** "La entrega por el comprador de una cantidad de dinero al vendedor se entiende hecha como arras confirmatorias, es decir, en señal de conclusión y a cuenta del precio de la compraventa." | 621-8.1 |
| **Penitential arras must be agreed expressly.** If the buyer withdraws, it loses them, unless the withdrawal is justified under art. 621-49; if the seller withdraws, it returns them doubled | 621-8.2 |
| **Registered penitential arras.** In a sale of real estate, penitential arras agreed for **at most six months** and **deposited with a notary** can be recorded in the Land Registry; the property is then liable for their return, and on withdrawal the notary hands them to whoever is entitled. The lien ends 60 days after the agreed term (unless the buyer has registered a claim), when the buyer withdraws and the seller proves it, or when the sale is registered | 621-8.3 |
| **Financing by a third party.** If the contract **provides for** financing of all or part of the price by a credit institution, the buyer, unless agreed otherwise, may withdraw by proving in writing, within the agreed term, that the **designated** institution refused the loan or the subrogation in the existing mortgage, unless the refusal is due to the buyer's negligence | 621-49.1 |
| On that withdrawal the seller returns the price received and, where relevant, the penitential arras | 621-49.2 |

What changes against the common law, and what to tell the user:

- The default (confirmatory) is **written in the law**, not only in case law.
- The financing protection applies by law when the contract **provides for** financing of the price by a credit institution: unless agreed otherwise, the buyer may withdraw by proving in writing, within the agreed term, that the institution refused, without negligence on the buyer's part. The article does not say the contract must name the bank. If the contract provides for financing but is silent on the institution or the term, say how 621-49 reads and send that point to the lawyer as "not verified". If the contract is silent on financing, 621-49 does not clearly apply: ask whether the parties later agreed any financing term or extended a financing deadline (an addendum, an email, a new date), and send the effect of that to the lawyer as "not verified". A clause "unless agreed otherwise" excluding it is **serious** for a buyer who needs a mortgage.
- The deposit with a notary and registration (621-8.3) is a protection the user can ask for, especially for large deposits.

### Community debts

Book Five (Ley 5/2006, property rights), consolidated text, last modified 3 Jul 2026: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-11130

| Rule | Article |
|---|---|
| The unit is liable for the community fees and reserve fund contributions owed by the current and former owners for the elapsed part of the current year and the **four** previous calendar years (common law: three) | 553-5.1 |
| The seller must declare being up to date or list what is owed, and provide a certificate from the community's secretary that also lists approved fees not yet due. Without it the deed cannot be executed, unless the parties expressly waive it. The seller remains liable for its debt | 553-5.2 |

### Housing law: information, off-plan and habitability

Ley 18/2007, del derecho a la vivienda, consolidated text, last modified 25 Mar 2026: https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2008-3657

| Rule | Article | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Information before any money.** The agents involved in building and real estate services (developers, agencies) must give the buyer in writing, **before any amount on account is paid**: who the agent is, the home and its useful surface, the registry reference, the total price with the taxes and costs the buyer bears, and for off-plan homes the start and delivery dates | 60.1-60.2 | [art. 60](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2008-3657#a60) |
| If the amount on account exceeds **1% of the price**, that information must also include, among others: the charges and encumbrances of the property and its participation quota, the community fees and the community debts pending (second-hand homes in horizontal property), the payment terms, and the building licence (first sale) | 60.3 | same |
| **Professional sellers.** When the sale is made within a business or professional activity, the clauses must be concrete, clear and simple, without references to documents not provided; unfair terms are excluded, and any non-negotiated clause that creates a significant imbalance against the consumer, that leaves the contract to the will of the business, or that **lacks reciprocity** is considered unfair; in case of doubt, the interpretation most favourable to the consumer prevails | 62.1.a-c (unfair terms: 62.1.b) | [art. 62](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2008-3657#a62) |
| **Off-plan: before signing or taking any money on account**, the seller must have the building licence, a right on the plot that allows building and selling (stating the charges on the home and the common elements), the legally required guarantees and insurance, and the mortgage loan split per unit where applicable | 63 | [art. 63](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2008-3657#a63) |
| **Habitability certificate (cédula de habitabilidad).** Any sale must prove the home meets the quality conditions by handing over the valid certificate | 26.2 | [art. 26](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2008-3657#a26) |
| The notary must require it before authorising the sale. In a sale of a home that is not new, the buyer can expressly exempt the seller only if the home is to be renovated (with a technical report showing it can obtain the certificate afterwards) or demolished, or if both parties state it will not be used as a home. Other regulations may add exceptions (for example for official protected housing or delayed delivery); they are not in this file, so mark them "not verified" and ask the lawyer before reporting a sale as blocked | 132.a | [art. 132](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2008-3657#a132) |

What to tell the user in Catalonia:

- Art. 60 applies when a developer or a real estate agency is involved, even if the seller is a private individual selling through the agency. Art. 62 applies when the seller sells as a business. Neither applies between two private individuals without an agency. If you do not know, ask (Step 2, question 4) or treat them as conditional.
- With a developer or an agency, a deposit taken **without the written information of art. 60** is a point for the lawyer. It is **serious** for a buyer when the price breakdown with taxes and costs is missing (required for any amount), or when the amount exceeds **1% of the price** and the charges are missing, or, for a second-hand home in horizontal property, the community fees and debts are missing (art. 60.3). Below 1%, a missing item from 60.3 is not a breach, and a new home with no community debts yet does not need that item.
- A one-sided penalty (the buyer loses the deposit, the seller has no mirror obligation) in a sale where the **seller sells as a business** is not only negotiable: under art. 62.1.b it may be an **unfair term** for lack of reciprocity. Mark it serious and add it to the questions for the lawyer. If the seller is a private individual using an agency's template, rate it on its own terms and add "does art. 62 apply to this sale? (not verified)" to the questions for the lawyer.
- Take the documents to request from the table below.

### Documents to request in Catalonia

Use this list instead of the common-law one: community debts follow a different rule here.

| Document | Why |
|---|---|
| Nota simple from the Land Registry, recent | Owner, charges (mortgages, seizures), co-owners |
| Certificate of debts with the community of owners, issued by its secretary | Art. 553-5: debts of the current year and the **four** previous ones follow the property; the certificate also lists approved fees not yet due. Without it the deed cannot be executed unless the parties expressly waive it |
| Last IBI receipt | TRLRHL art. 64: unpaid IBI follows the property |
| Habitability certificate (cédula de habitabilidad), valid | Ley 18/2007 arts. 26.2 and 132.a: required to sell, with the exceptions of 132.a; other exceptions may exist (not verified) |
| Energy performance certificate | Usually required to sell; no article in this file, so cite it as "not verified" |
| From a developer or an agency: the written information of art. 60 | Required before any payment on account; above 1% of the price it must include the charges and, for a second-hand home in horizontal property, the community fees and debts |
| If off-plan: the licence, the guarantee (insurance or bank guarantee) and the special account | Ley 18/2007 art. 63 and LOE DA 1ª |
| If the seller is represented: the power of attorney | Who signs must be able to bind the owner |

## Other territories with their own civil law

Aragón, Navarra, País Vasco, Galicia and the Balearic Islands have their own civil law (derecho foral o especial). This skill has **no reference** for them. For a property there:

- Say that their own rules may apply to the sale and to the arras, and that this skill does not cover them.
- Apply the jurisdiction-agnostic analysis and the state laws that apply everywhere (LOE DA 1ª, Ley 7/2012). The TRLRHL (IBI, plusvalía) applies in Aragón, Galicia and the Balearic Islands, but not as such in Navarra and País Vasco: art. 1.2 TRLRHL leaves their foral financial regimes intact. There, mark IBI and plusvalía as "not verified" and add them to the questions for the lawyer. Present the common-law rules on arras only as "common civil law, may not be the applicable rule here".
- Add the applicable regional rule to the questions for the lawyer.

Common law applies in the rest of Spain, including the Valencian Community and the Canary Islands for these purposes; if the user disputes that, treat it as a point for the lawyer.
